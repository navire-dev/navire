# Code of Conduct

Navire is committed to maintaining a respectful, constructive, and inclusive
community for everyone who participates in the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith
- focus on the technical subject being discussed
- provide constructive feedback
- accept different viewpoints and technical experience levels
- take responsibility for mistakes and correct them when possible

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- abusive, insulting, or deliberately disruptive communication
- publishing private information without permission
- sexualized language or unwanted advances
- any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional technical community

## Reporting concerns

Please report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a
private GitHub channel. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to the
[security policy](.github/SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Enforcement decisions will be based on the behavior and its impact
on the project community.
